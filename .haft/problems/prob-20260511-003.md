# Forward uptime alerts to client email

## Signal

Netdata uptime alerts currently fire to owner only. Client needs visibility into outages without requiring access to Netdata instance or phone app.

## Problem Type

search

## Constraints

- Client must not need a Netdata account or app install
- Alert content must be legible to a non-technical client (not raw Netdata JSON)
- Must not expose Netdata credentials or dashboard URL to client
- Cannot add paid alerting SaaS

## Optimization Targets

- Client receives alert within 2 minutes of outage (same SLA as owner alert)
- Minimal ongoing maintenance (set-and-forget forwarding)

## Observation Indicators

- False positive rate seen by client (noisy alerts damage trust)

## Acceptance

- Client receives email when koiiswim.com is down
- Client receives all-clear email when site recovers
- Owner can disable client forwarding without disrupting own alerts

## Blast Radius

- Netdata alert config on monitoring instance
- Client inbox (external)

## Reversibility

high — remove forwarding rule or recipient to revert
