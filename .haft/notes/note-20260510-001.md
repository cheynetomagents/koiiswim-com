# Expand baseline scope to include SEO and AI optimization metrics

## Rationale

SEO metrics (meta tags, structured data, sitemap, canonical URLs, crawlability) and AI optimization metrics (llms.txt, structured content, schema markup for LLM discoverability) are directly impacted by SRE changes. Capturing them now ensures we can measure regression or improvement post-change. Alternative was performance-only baseline — rejected because SEO/AI signals are harder to attribute after-the-fact.
